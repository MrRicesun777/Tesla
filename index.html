Interior

The first supercar to combine record-breaking performance with refined comfort. The cabin is meticulously crafted with premium materials, ambient lighting, and advanced technology – all designed to deliver an unmatched driving experience.

Experience freedom like never before. The lightweight, removable glass roof transforms the vehicle into a true convertible within seconds. Enjoy open-air driving while still benefiting from aerodynamic design and structural rigidity.
